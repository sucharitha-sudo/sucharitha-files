# SaaS Platform Documentation

## Introduction

I worked on the Matchbook AI, which is a SaaS-based enterprise data intelligence platform that helps organizations manage, cleanse, match, and enrich their business data.

I contributed to creating and maintaining technical and marketing documentation for the Matchbook AI platform, helping users understand its features, functionalities, and processes.

My documentation contributions included:

* Technical documentation and user guides
* Product release notes
* Technical and business white papers
* Case studies
* Capability statements
* Video scripts
* A series of blogs for the product's AWS Marketplace launch
* FTR (Foundational Technical Review) documentation

Tools & Technologies: 

* Documentation & Editing: **Visual Studio Code**
* Version Control & Collaboration: **GitHub**
* Diagramming & Visualization: **Draw.io**
* AI Assistant Platform: **Alltius**
* Screen Capture & Documentation: **Snagit**
* Project Management & Issue Tracking: **Jira**

To learn more about the platform I contributed to documenting, visit: [Matchbook AI](https://www.matchbookai.com/)
