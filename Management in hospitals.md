# Management in hospitals

## Introduction
This blog discusses how patients behave in hospitals, their attitudes towards doctors and staff, and their expectations from healthcare services. It highlights common situations involving complaints about medical expenses, delays in checkups, impatience, and disagreements with hospital staff.
